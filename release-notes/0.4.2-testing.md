## Games
- **Time played and back to the dashboard.** Games started from the dashboard are timed, and the dashboard comes back when they close (Settings > Games to turn it off). Steam games count from when Steam says they're running.
- **More places to bring games in from:** Epic, GOG, Lutris and itch.io (Heroic's Epic and GOG games too), PS2 discs through PCSX2, Linux app shortcuts, and icons straight out of .exe, .dll and .ico files. xemu and PCSX2 get found in a lot more places, RetroBat and Flatpak included.
- **Your own collections,** shown in the launcher next to Favorites. Filter a list by where games come from (X), sort All Games by time or last played (Y), and search by name.
- **Per-game options:** arguments, working folder and environment for any game.
- First launch offers to import your Steam games and find your emulators.

## Controllers
- Any number of pads, plugged in or out while it runs.
- PlayStation and Nintendo pads get their own button prompts, and there's a setting to confirm with the right button.
- Rumble when the guide can't do something (Settings > Controller turns it off).

## Jellyfin and Plex
- Continue Watching and Next Up, resume where you stopped (X starts from the beginning), and your progress goes back to the server.
- An info card on Y with year, runtime, rating, genres and the plot. Watched marks in lists, RB to mark watched, and search.
- Folders and box sets open, episodes read "S1E05 · Title", and you see Loading or Can't reach the server instead of an empty list.
- Fixed posters that never showed up, the header running into the item count, the pad doing nothing during a video, and B backing out of the menu as well as the video.
- Sign-in tokens no longer end up in theseus.log.

## Everything else
- The guide reads better: only things you can do are buttons, information is plain text, and pages are split into sections.
- **Back Up My Settings** and **Restore a Backup** in Logs & Help.
- **Portable mode:** put a `portable.txt` next to the app and everything stays in a `UserData` folder beside it.
- Music keeps track order when files aren't tagged, and .m3u playlists keep their order (#27).
- Copy Log hides sign-ins along with your home folder and IP addresses.

Plex support is written but hasn't been tried against a real server yet. Same for the store imports on real installs. If something's off, the issue form on GitHub plus `theseus.log` (Guide > Logs & Help > Copy Log) is the fastest way to get it fixed.
